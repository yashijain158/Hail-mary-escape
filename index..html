🚀 HAIL MARY ESCAPE

The Hail Mary is crippled. Astrophage lasers, a dark hull and a dead flight computer stand between you and Tau Ceti. Rocky is counting on you.

Stage 1 – cross the Astrophage laser grid (touch = +3:00 penalty, 3 touches = mission failed), scan the QR, enter the code.
Stage 2 – dark room: find 4 hidden “+” markers with your torch, build the code.
Stage 3 – disassemble the PC, read the code, enter it to finish.
Your 3 crewmates (Rocky, Stratt, Yao) play simultaneously – see the board.

WASD / arrows = move · Shift = run · drag mouse = look · click = interact
